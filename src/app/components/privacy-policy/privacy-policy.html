🛕 About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit www.shyamjisewasamiti.org, make donations, contact us, volunteer or participate in our seva activities.

📋 Information We Collect

We may collect your name, mobile number, email address, postal address, PAN number, donation amount, campaign selected, payment reference, transaction ID, messages, and information submitted through website forms.

💝 Donation & Receipt Data

Donation information is used for payment verification, receipt generation, donor communication, internal accounting, legal compliance and maintaining transparent records of seva activities.

🔐 Payment Security

Online payments are processed through authorized payment gateway partners such as Razorpay or banking partners. We do not store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking password or sensitive banking credentials.

🧾 Tax & Compliance Information

Where required, PAN, address and donation records may be collected for receipt generation, audit, legal, accounting and applicable tax-related compliance.

📞 Communication

We may contact you through phone, email, SMS or WhatsApp for donation confirmation, receipt sharing, seva updates, campaign information or support requests. Promotional messages will be limited and relevant.

🍪 Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to improve website performance, user experience, security, page speed and service quality. You may control cookies through your browser settings.

🤝 Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services including payment gateways, hosting providers, analytics tools, map services, email/SMS providers and social media platforms. Their own policies may apply separately.

🛡 Data Protection

We use reasonable technical, administrative and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, disclosure or destruction.

⏳ Data Retention

We retain donation and donor records for as long as required for legal, accounting, audit, receipt, dispute resolution and organizational purposes.

👶 Children’s Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children below 13 years. If such information is found, we may delete it after verification.

🔗 External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites.

📩 Your Rights

You may contact us to request correction, update or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal, audit and compliance requirements.

📝 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated information will be published on this page with the latest revision date.
