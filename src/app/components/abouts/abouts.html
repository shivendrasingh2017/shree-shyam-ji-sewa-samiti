🕉 Shyam Ji Sewa Samiti

Bhakti, Sewa aur
Manav Kalyan ka Sankalp

Shyam Ji Sewa Samiti ek dharmik aur samajik sewa sanstha hai. Hamara uddeshya bhakton ko Baba Shyam aur Mahadev ki bhakti se jodna, zaruratmand logon ki sahayata karna aur samaj me sewa ki bhavna ko badhava dena hai.

Bhagwan Shiv tyag, daya, shakti aur kalyan ke prateek hain. Isi prerna se hamari samiti bhandara, puja, aarti, bhajan sandhya, gau sewa, food sewa aur garib parivaron ki madad jaise karyon me nirantar yogdan deti hai.
