🕉 Shree Shyam Ji Sewa Samiti

Bhakti, Seva aur
Manav Kalyan ka Sankalp

Shree Shyam Ji Sewa Samiti ek dharmik aur samajik seva sanstha hai. Hamara uddeshya bhakton ko Baba Shyam aur Mahadev ki bhakti se jodna, zaruratmand logon ki sahayata karna aur samaj me seva ki bhavna ko badhava dena hai.

Bhagwan Shiv tyag, daya, shakti aur kalyan ke prateek hain. Isi prerna se hamari samiti bhandara, puja, aarti, bhajan sandhya, gau seva, food seva aur garib parivaron ki madad jaise karyon me nirantar yogdan deti hai.
